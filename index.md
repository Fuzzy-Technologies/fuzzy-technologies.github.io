## What we build

We build engineering products for problems where software has to do real work: preserve state, automate workflows, handle uncertainty, and produce results that can be checked.

<div class="grid project-grid">
  <article class="card featured">
    <span class="card-label">Security · AI · Open Source</span>
    <h3>🛡️ 1337 Security Workbench</h3>
    <p>An open security workstation for security engineers, penetration testers, and security teams — with an architecture designed for future AI security agents as well.</p>
    <p>1337 brings scope, security objects, controlled execution, evidence, reachability, and attack-path reasoning into one workspace.</p>
    <p class="quiet">Early pre-alpha · Apache-2.0 Community core</p>
    <div class="project-links">
      <a href="https://fuzzy-technologies.github.io/1337/">Project site →</a>
      <a href="https://github.com/Fuzzy-Technologies/1337">GitHub →</a>
    </div>
  </article>

  <article class="card">
    <span class="card-label">Quant · Analytics · Trading</span>
    <h3>📊 Fuzzy Market Analytics</h3>
    <p>A family of quantitative analytics and trading systems built around statistics, probability models, anomaly filtering, risk estimation, and fuzzy inference.</p>
    <p>The focus is on interpretable rules, measurable risk, and real market data rather than promises of a perfect forecast.</p>
    <div class="project-links">
      <a href="https://fuzzy-technologies.github.io/FMA/en/">Project page (RU) →</a>
    </div>
  </article>

    <h3>⚙️ TKSBrokerAPI</h3>
    <p>An open-source Python platform for the T-Investments API, market-data collection and enrichment, analytics, and automated trading scenarios.</p>
    <p>It is used as an infrastructure layer for part of our quantitative and trading work.</p>
